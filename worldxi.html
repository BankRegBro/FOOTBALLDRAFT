How to Play
World XI · 2026 World Cup Draft
The Drill
  • First, roll a formation (4-3-3, 4-4-2, 4-2-3-1, 3-5-2, 3-4-3 or 5-3-2). It sets the shape of your starting XI on the pitch and is locked for the game.
  • Then roll a nation. Every one of the 48 World Cup teams comes up with the same, equal odds each roll.
  • Draft one player from that nation into an open slot, or hit Reroll for a different nation. Reroll as often as you like.
  • Fill all sixteen spots: the eleven of your formation, plus a five-man bench (a sub keeper and four outfield subs).
  • Every slot takes its own position; the four bench outfield spots take any outfielder. When the squad is full it grades out of 100 and projects a World Cup run.
  • You cannot draft the same player twice.
What's Scored
  • Slots are weighted. The striker and keeper carry a small premium, and the starting XI counts for far more than the bench.
  • There is no position-fit bonus: a player rates the same wherever they are eligible to play.
  • Every squad is built from real internationals, so the raw average always runs high. The club rating stretches that band onto a full 40–100 scale, which is why a blind draft bows out in the group and a sharp one can win it.
  • Because rerolls are unlimited and every nation has plenty of players, you can never get stuck — if a nation does not fit, just reroll.
Modes & the Hall of Fame
  • Difficulty: Blitz hides ratings while you draft. Rookie marks elite players with a star as you pick. The final score is identical either way; Blitz is simply blind.
  • The Hall of Fame records Blitz runs. It shares one global board with the rest of the arcade. Rookie still plays and scores, but will not post.
